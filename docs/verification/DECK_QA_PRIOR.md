# Faculty slides — visual QA findings

Inspected all nine rendered previews in `upi-review-integration-01/execution-evidence/deck-preview/slide-{1..9}.jpg` using the requested Office visual-QA checks. No edits were made.

## Findings

- **Slide 6 — low contrast:** The chart heading “Legitimate GE mimics” is rendered in near-black on the charcoal background and is effectively unreadable at presentation size. The plotted bars, axes, and large adverse benign percentages remain legible.
- **Slide 7 — low contrast:** The three underlined literature links (“Fraud-RLA,” “Banking evasion,” “PaySim”) use dark blue on the dark background. They are difficult to read and less visible than the rest of the slide text.
- **Slide 8 — unreadable embedded interface:** The workstation screenshot is too small for its interface labels or data to be read at the displayed size. It works as a visual illustration, but not as legible evidence of the interface.
- **Slide 9 — tight footer clearance:** The second gold takeaway sits very close to the gray project-record source line below it. It does not visibly overlap, but the gap is cramped.
- **Campaign total — not stated explicitly:** The previews show “360 campaigns per arm/detector” on slide 5 and “3,600 per detector” for ordinary benign on slide 6, but do not state the expected **7,200 total campaigns** directly. The total must be inferred across the slides.
